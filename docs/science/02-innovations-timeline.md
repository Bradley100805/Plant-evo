# 02. Key innovations timeline

**Status:** v0. Phase 0 checked the plan's dates against the literature. Each innovation gets a
full model specification (developmental basis, physiology hooks, validation scenario) in Phase 7.

This chapter is the reference timeline for the campaign (plan §6.2) and the innovations catalog
(plan §5). The game never forces these dates: lineages evolve innovations when the model allows.
The timeline is what players' histories are compared against.

## Verified first appearances

Ages are approximate. "First appearance" means the oldest widely accepted fossil evidence unless
marked as a molecular-clock estimate. Fossil first appearances are minimum ages: an innovation
can be older than its oldest fossil.

| Innovation / event                   | First evidence                                                                | Age                                                                                 | Confidence                                  | Source                                                 |
| ------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| Origin of land plants (embryophytes) | Molecular clock                                                               | Middle Cambrian–Early Ordovician (~515–470 Ma)                                      | Well supported (wide uncertainty)           | Morris et al. 2018                                     |
| Land-plant spores (cryptospores)     | Dapingian cryptospores, Argentina                                             | ~473–471 Ma                                                                         | Well supported                              | Rubinstein et al. 2010                                 |
| Vascular plant macrofossils          | _Cooksonia_, mid-Silurian                                                     | ~430 Ma                                                                             | Well supported                              | Kenrick & Crane 1997 (review); primary source to add   |
| Stomata                              | Silurian and Early Devonian cuticles and permineralizations                   | Silurian (exact oldest record not pinned)                                           | Well supported                              | Edwards, Kerp & Hass 1998                              |
| Rhynie chert ecosystem               | Hot-spring deposit, Scotland                                                  | ~407 Ma (Ar–Ar) or ~411.5 Ma (U–Pb); see note 1                                     | Established (site); dates debated           | Mark et al. 2011; Parry et al. 2011                    |
| Arbuscular mycorrhizae               | Arbuscules in _Aglaophyton_, Rhynie chert                                     | ~407 Ma                                                                             | Established                                 | Remy et al. 1994                                       |
| Roots                                | Independent origins in lycophytes and euphyllophytes                          | Devonian                                                                            | Well supported                              | Hetherington & Dolan 2018                              |
| Wood (secondary xylem)               | Two small Early Devonian euphyllophytes                                       | 407–397 Ma                                                                          | Well supported                              | Gerrienne et al. 2011                                  |
| Heterospory                          | Graded spore sizes (_Omniastrobus_); true heterospory appears Emsian–Givetian | ~400 Ma; 408–383 Ma for the main appearance                                         | Well supported                              | Bonacorsi et al. 2020; Bonacorsi & Leslie 2022         |
| Heterospory, number of origins       | Comparative analysis                                                          | At least ~11 independent origins                                                    | Well supported                              | Bateman & DiMichele 1994                               |
| Seed precursor                       | _Runcaria_, Belgium (cupulate megasporangium)                                 | ~385 Ma (Givetian)                                                                  | Well supported                              | Gerrienne et al. 2004                                  |
| Seeds (preovules)                    | _Elkinsia_, _Moresnetia_ and others                                           | Late Devonian (Famennian, ~365 Ma); exact date not yet checked                      | Well supported                              | Gerrienne et al. 2004 (context); primary source to add |
| Megaphylls and leaf broadening       | Planate leaves spread as CO₂ fell                                             | Late Devonian                                                                       | Hypothesis (CO₂ link); Established (timing) | Beerling, Osborne & Chaloner 2001                      |
| Flowering plants (angiosperms)       | Earliest widely accepted pollen                                               | Valanginian–Hauterivian (~135–130 Ma)                                               | Well supported; older claims disputed       | Doyle 2012                                             |
| C4 photosynthesis, first origins     | Grass lineages, molecular dating                                              | ~32–25 Ma (Oligocene)                                                               | Well supported                              | Christin et al. 2008; Vicentini et al. 2008            |
| C4 photosynthesis, number of origins | Comparative survey                                                            | At least 62 lineages (later counts 61–66)                                           | Established                                 | Sage, Christin & Edwards 2011                          |
| C4 grassland expansion               | Carbon isotopes in soils and teeth                                            | Late Miocene (~8–5 Ma)                                                              | Established                                 | Cerling et al. 1997                                    |
| Polyploid speciation                 | Comparative survey                                                            | About 15% of angiosperm and 31% of fern speciation events involve a ploidy increase | Well supported                              | Wood et al. 2009                                       |

**Note 1, Rhynie chert age.** Parry et al. (2011) obtained 411.5 ± 1.3 Ma from U–Pb zircon
dating of an associated lava. Mark et al. (2011) obtained 407.1 ± 2.2 Ma (U–Pb-comparable Ar–Ar).
The two groups disputed the Ar–Ar calibration in 2013. The game should present the Rhynie chert
as "about 407–411 million years old" until this chapter pins one value with a reason.

## Corrections to the plan

Phase 0 changed these entries in plan §5:

| Entry           | Plan v0 said | Now says                                              | Why                                                        |
| --------------- | ------------ | ----------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Earliest spores | ~470 Ma      | ~473–471 Ma (Dapingian)                               | Rubinstein et al. 2010                                     |
| Stomata         | by ~420 Ma   | Silurian                                              | No primary source found yet that pins a single oldest date |
| Heterospory     | ~385 Ma      | ~400 Ma (graded spores); 408–383 Ma (main appearance) | Bonacorsi et al. 2020; Bonacorsi & Leslie 2022             |
| Rhynie chert    | ~407 Ma      | ~407–411 Ma                                           | Two dating methods disagree (note 1)                       |

## Not yet checked

To be verified in Phase 7, before each innovation is implemented: microphylls, branched
sporophyte (polysporangiophytes), pollen and pollen tubes (siphonogamy), vessels and their
convergent origins outside angiosperms, fruits, CAM photosynthesis (origins and timing),
deciduousness and dormancy.
